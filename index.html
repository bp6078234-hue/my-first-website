Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about future IT skills? Find answers to the most common queries below.

Which skill should I start learning first as a beginner?

Full Stack Development is a great starting point because it gives you immediate visual feedback through HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Once you build strong logic, you can easily transition into AI, Cloud, or Cybersecurity.

Do I need a Computer Science degree to master these skills?

No, a formal CS degree is not strictly required. The IT industry highly values practical skills, hands-on projects, problem-solving ability, and continuous learning through online courses and certifications.

How long does it take to become job-ready in Full Stack or AI?

With dedicated learning of 2 to 3 hours daily, it usually takes around 6 to 9 months to build solid foundational skills and portfolio projects that make you ready for entry-level roles.

Where can I find free resources to learn these skills?

You can find high-quality free tutorials on YouTube channels, freeCodeCamp, MDN Web Docs, and official documentation of tech stacks like AWS, Python, and React.
